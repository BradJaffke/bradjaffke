#Who Am I?

Hi!  My name is Brad Jaffke.  I am a passionate Software Solutions Architect working at Liberty Mutual Insurance in Seattle, WA.  I started my career working in legacy IBM technologies (BPM, WAS, and WAS-LP), and transitioned to cloud-native development in 2017.  I currently lead 2 teams of engineers working in the central architecture department at Liberty Mutual.  We provide production-ready starter patterns for the entire enterprise and enable other engineering teams to work faster and get their products out to market in a more efficient manner while also ensuring that they are using the best and most secure technologies and tools.

#What sorts of coding do I do?

I started my career writing a lot of Java.  When I transitioned to Cloud-native development I added Spring on and that is certainly my expertise at this point.  However, over the last couple years I have added a lot of breadth to my toolkit and have dabbled in Javascript (Node, React, Angular), Python, GraphQL, and others.  I feel that a good software engineer never stops learning, and I embrace that myself.
